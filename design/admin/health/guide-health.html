3Device health

What it does

It shows how the device is doing right now. It is only for looking. You cannot change anything here.

When to look at it

When readings are missing, or the page says Offline. It helps you find out why.

What each line means

LineWhat it tells you
Wi-Fi signalHow strong the connection is. Good and Fair are fine. Weak can make readings go missing.
Last restartWhy the device last started. See the list below.
FirmwareThe version of the program on the device. Tell it to the person who set up the device when you ask for help.
Last reportWhen the device last sent a reading. Late means more than 2 minutes ago.

If the signal is Weak

Move the Wi-Fi hotspot closer to the device, or the device closer to the hotspot. Then look again after a few minutes.

ExampleThe number is in dBm, and closer to 0 is stronger. −58 dBm is stronger than −81 dBm.

If you see a restart reason

It saysWhat to do
Power onNothing. Someone plugged the device in.
Reset pin, Software restartNothing, if someone pressed the reset button or updated the device.
Power dippedThe power was too low for a moment. Check the cable and the power bank.
Stopped responding, CrashThe device froze and restarted by itself. If it keeps happening, tell the person who set up the device.
Not reported yet means the device has not sent its health. This is normal until it gets its next update.
