Heat alerts

Get a notification on this phone when the classroom gets dangerously hot.

  • When the heat index in Room 204 reaches 42 °C (Danger).
  • If it stays that hot, a reminder comes every 60 minutes.

On iPhone, add Init to your Home Screen first, then open it from there.

  1. Tap the Share button (□↑).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Init from the Home Screen and come back here.
