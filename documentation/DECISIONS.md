| D-018 | **No deep sleep.** The device stays awake and reads, shows and (from P03) sends continuously. Deep sleep is dropped from the design for now | Owner approved, 2026-10-31 | Bench test P01-04: the ROMOSS Sense 8P+ stayed on about 57 s (the awake phase) and switched off when the board entered deep sleep, also in its low-current mode. No USB meter is available and the deadline is close, so the always-awake fallback (about 90 mA, roughly 8 days per charge, an estimate) is chosen. Deep sleep can return with a different power source. P01-07 (currents) is deferred until a meter is available. |
