# Phase 8 — Over-the-air updates (optional)

**Objective:** Update the device firmware without a cable, without risking a bricked device.
**Entry criteria:** P07 closed and the owner decides to do this.
**Exit criteria:** A signed update installs on the real device, and a deliberately bad update rolls back.
**Features:** F-19. Model: Opus 5.5.

Checklist markers: see phase-01.

- [ ] P08-01 Design written and approved by the owner (signing, rollback, how an update is published)
- [ ] P08-02 Signed update installs on the device
- [ ] P08-03 A bad update is rejected or rolled back, tested on the real device
