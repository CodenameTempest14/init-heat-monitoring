# paper/data/

Aggregates of the 24 h run (2026-11-02 10:03:55Z to 2026-11-03 10:03:55Z, init-test, one device): `summary.json`,
`hourly.json` (Philippine time), `bands.json` (PAGASA levels), `gaps.json` (gaps over 90 s). Made by `../fetch-data.mjs`.
No keys, no personal data.

`screens/`: three cropped phone screenshots (home online, home offline, heat alerts page; owner's Android, light mode, 5 Nov 2026) used in Figure 7. No personal data.
