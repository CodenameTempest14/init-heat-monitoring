# enclosure/

The 3D printed case for the Init device (P07-01, decision D-038): **Option A**, a two-part case.
A main box holds the ESP32 and the OLED; the DHT22 sits in its own vented pod on a short cable;
the power bank has its own pocket behind a divider wall. Printed on a Bambu Lab A1 in PETG.

Files:
- `MEASUREMENTS.md`: the sheet the owner fills in with calipers (all in mm). The design waits for it.

Later: parametric OpenSCAD files, exported `.stl` files, preview images, `PRINT_GUIDE.md`.
OpenSCAD (Nightly 2026.10.31) is at `C:\Program Files\OpenSCAD (Nightly)\openscad.exe`.
